# Third-party material

This repository ships no third-party source texts. Fixtures are original or public domain. The Mouseia worked example reads from a separate checkout; its corpus is not copied into this repository. References to Mouseia file paths document evidence, not a license grant to redistribute those files.

Original teaching text composed for the documentation is not a historical source quotation. The original Latin exercise in `docs/authoring.md` is expressly dedicated to the public domain there; the surrounding documentation and illustrative English renderings remain CC BY 4.0. Fixture and example records must identify original authorship or the public-domain basis. Do not assume an ancient underlying work makes a provider's transcription public domain.

The reference implementation has no runtime dependencies outside the Python standard library. Its MIT license and the specification/documentation's CC BY 4.0 license do not replace the rights of any source a host project acquires. Hosts retain separate underlying-work and transcription rights, attribution, share-alike obligations, and evidence of compatibility (SRC-4, PRV-5; mechanical record checks, judgment of rights). The checker inspects those records; it cannot certify legal status or translation sense.

## Report a licensing problem

Open an issue at [the repository issue tracker](https://github.com/michael-berardi/champollion/issues) with the exact repository path and revision, the material at issue, the claimed rightsholder or original source, and the evidence of conflicting rights or missing attribution. Cite the problematic passage rather than redistributing a full source export. Include the fixture or example identifier when applicable.

The maintainer will review the evidence and correct attribution, replace the material, or remove it as appropriate. A source with unknown rights must not be admitted merely to keep a check green (SRC-4; mechanical rights gate, judgment of rights determination). Record any resulting behavior change with its fixture and changelog entry.
